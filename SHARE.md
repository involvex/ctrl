## Audience

People who run `@involvex/ctrl` on Android with on-device LiteRT, or who want a local-first phone agent without a PC.

## Announcement

`@involvex/ctrl` Model setup on phone can keep several `.litertlm` files in an app-owned catalog. Switching models unloads the current LiteRT engine and loads the next on demand (still one engine in RAM — replace-to-switch, not dual load). Download the default Gemma E2B or import files from storage; GGUF stays on desktop. Repo: https://github.com/involvex/ctrl
